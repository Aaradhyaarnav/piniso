# ✏️ Piniso
### Your funny AI study buddy for YouTube lectures

A cute 3D blob with a pencil that sits on your lecture, writes your notes, quizzes you, roasts you when you open Reels… and somehow makes you study.

**Free · Edge + Chrome · Hindi + English · by Eka**

## ✨ What it does

| | Feature | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 📝 | **Auto notes** | Notes write themselves while the lecture plays |
| ⭐ | **Star on important** | Teacher says "ye important hai" → Piniso stars it |
| ❓ | **Pop quizzes** | MCQ, fill in the blanks, short answers. Skippable (he remembers 👀) |
| 🎙 | **Voice mode** | Ask out loud — he answers out loud. Fluent **हिंदी + English** |
| 📘 | **PDF booklet** | One click → mind map, formula sheet, practice questions, answer key |
| 🛡 | **Guardian** | Open Instagram or Shorts while studying? Blocked. Sorry not sorry |
| 🎨 | **Make him yours** | 10 hats, glasses, colours, 22 moods, 67 animations |

## ⬇️ Install in 2 minutes

1. Click the green **Code** button above → **Download ZIP** → unzip it.
2. Open **`edge://extensions`** (Edge) or **`chrome://extensions`** (Chrome).
3. Turn on **Developer mode**.
4. Click **Load unpacked** → select the unzipped `piniso` folder (the one with `manifest.json` inside).
5. The welcome page opens. Paste a **free Groq key** from [console.groq.com/keys](https://console.groq.com/keys) (or use Chrome's built-in AI).
6. Open any YouTube lecture → press **✨ Piniso it** → study.

> Your notes stay on your computer. Piniso only uses the AI key **you** give it.
